### 📣 My proudest projects:

> [Odoo-l10n_se_payroll](https://github.com/vertelab/odoo-l10n_se_payroll):
_A Swedish Odoo-payroll module which I had the opportunity to spend the better part of my last 4-month internship period updating and developing. It now conforms with Odoo 18, as well as current Swedish salary laws and regulations._

> [Odoo-dms/dms_webpage/daemon](https://github.com/vertelab/odoo-document/tree/18.0/dms_webpage/daemon):
_A Daemon-script which allows the user to sync a local folder from their filesystem with their Odoo DMS-filestore in the web UI._

> [PantryPioneer](https://github.com/JamieZiegler/PantryPioneer):
_Maybe I sort of have to list this one even though it isn't finished quite yet? It is my graduation project, a digital pantry with recipe-matching functionality. And I feel like it will be something I am rather proud of in the end._

> [MatPaFat](https://github.com/JamieZiegler/MatPaFat): 
_This is a, perhaps not so technically advanced, but highly successful, website for a new catering business._
